Rasf is a software and design studio from Syria. We build websites, web apps and online stores end to end, from the first idea to launch, and design the brands they carry.

## What we'll write about

This blog is our working notebook. We'll write about what we learn while we build:

- How we plan a project before the first line of code.
- Technical decisions we made, and why.
- Designing Arabic interfaces, from typefaces to page direction.

## Why the name

*Rasf* is laying stones one beside the next until they make a road. That's how we see building: detail after detail, nothing random.
